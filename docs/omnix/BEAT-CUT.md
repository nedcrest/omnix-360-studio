# Cutting to the beat — a view of its own (gap 57, for later)

The user's idea, 2026-11-10, kept here so it isn't lost; not being built.
Gap 13 (the beats found in a song, drawn on the music lane, snapping to
them) comes first and is the ground this stands on.

## What the user described

A view of its own **in place of the player**. With the right key the
user marks one music clip and one or more video clips on the timeline;
the view opens on them and helps cut the video to the music.

Where the timeline holds a long stretch of video
`--------------------------------------------`, the view makes it
shorter and cut: `--/--/----/-/-/-/----`.

Example: following Mar'yana as she walks (B-roll), then a walk through
the town (B-roll), music under both. The two walks and the music clip
are marked and go into the view.

The cutting comes in formats, all of them configurable. With more strong
beats chosen, the cut can be: a second's jump forward, then a long
stretch, then a few quick jumps forward again.

## How it meets what's recorded (gap 57)

- 57a the beats (gap 13 first: the analyser's lines and the user's own
  strong beats);
- 57b the ways of cutting: in a row, separate scenes, by intensity, a
  held moment; the user's «jumps forward» is one of them;
- 57c each piece starting on something worth seeing (motion, faces, an
  optional local model);
- 57d the result plain cuts on the timeline, editable, one undo step.
